01 // Profile & Engineering Philosophy

Design With Manufacturing in Mind From Day One

A disciplined engineering approach ensuring seamless translation from early conceptual sketches to validated production tooling.

I am a mechanical design engineer and certified SolidWorks specialist with a degree from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Taxila. My engineering practice is founded on a core principle: a CAD model is only as valuable as its manufacturability.

Throughout my 4+ years working with international hardware startups, machinery manufacturers, and consumer product founders, I have avoided pretty but unbuildable geometry. Every wall thickness is vetted for injection molding, every sheet metal flange respects standard bend radii and k-factors, and every CNC-machined surface includes proper tool clearance and GD&T tolerances.

From automated machinery and industrial conveyor lines to wearable electronics and agricultural robotics, I collaborate closely with clients to minimize design revisions, tooling delays, and unexpected fabrication expenses.
